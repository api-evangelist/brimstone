Brimstone has hired John Uhrie as the company’s Vice President of Engineering. John brings three decades of metallurgical expertise at major and startup mining companies alike. After working for companies such as Freeport-McMoRan and Newmont Corporation, he served as COO of Energy Fuels Inc.
